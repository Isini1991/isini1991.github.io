    Questions

    What do you need from me to start?

    A short note about the work and who it is for, a few sites you like, and any images or text you already have. We can shape the rest together.

    How long does a website take?

    It depends on the size. A focused portfolio is quicker than a shop or an archive. Tell me your timing and I will say honestly if it fits.

    Can I update the site myself?

    Yes. Where it makes sense I set up a simple CMS and show you how to use it.

    Do you work remotely?

    Yes. Projects run online, with calls at times that suit us both.

    What does a website cost?

    Every project is different. Send a few details and I will reply with a clear quote.
